# ISC sponsorship tiers

Four categories, by value contributed. Source: *Annex I, Sponsorship categories,
ISC FS Racing Team*.

> **Notion is canonical.** The Sponsorship Tiers page in the ISC Notion workspace
> (page `3ccd2857-f77c-817b-b99f-f7b1b2daeb84`) is the live version. Read it before
> building a proposal. If it disagrees with this file, Notion wins and this file is
> out of date. This copy exists so outreach drafting works without a round trip.

## Title Sponsor — above 30,000 EUR

- Logo size: **XL**
- Integration: car, driver equipment, team material
- Positions: side, top and front views

Benefits:

- Choice of the car's colour; the team contacts them for preferences
- Presence across all communication channels: events, media platforms, university campus
- Sponsor Day: live show of the car
- Access to the CVs and profiles of all team members

## Main Sponsor — above 15,000 EUR

- Logo size: **L**
- Integration: car, team material, team merchandise
- Positions: side, top and front views, plus rear wing backside

Benefits:

- Limited input on the car's colour alongside the logo
- Presence across all communication channels: events, media platforms, university campus
- Sponsor Day: live show of the car
- Access to the CVs and profiles of all team members

## Official Sponsor — above 7,500 EUR

- Logo size: **M**
- Integration: car, team material, team merchandise

Benefits:

- Presence across all communication channels: events, media platforms, university campus
- Sponsor Day: live show of the car

**Software and tool sponsorship sits in this category regardless of value.** This may
change if the contribution is made in cash rather than in licences or tools. When
pitching a software or tooling company, offer Official terms even if the licence value
is below or above the bracket.

## Associate Sponsor — below 7,500 EUR

- Logo size: **S**
- Integration: car, team merchandise

Benefits:

- Presence across all communication channels: events, media platforms, university campus

## Rules that apply to every tier

- **Logo position depends on the form of the contribution.** Equipment, supplies, and
  funds are weighted differently and the team distributes positions accordingly. Never
  promise a specific position on the car in writing without board agreement.
- Vehicle renders shown to sponsors are examples. The car is subject to change, and
  aerodynamic changes can move logo positions. Say so when sending renders.
- Never promise category exclusivity unless the board has explicitly agreed it.
- Never promise placement that conflicts with Formula Student rules on livery and
  mandatory markings.

## Choosing a tier for a prospect

Round down to the tier the contribution actually clears. A company offering 14,000 EUR
is an Official Sponsor, not a Main Sponsor. If the board makes an exception, record the
reason on the prospect's Notion page so the next person understands why the tier does
not match the value.

For in-kind offers, estimate the market price of what is being contributed and apply
the same brackets, except for software and tooling, which is always Official.
