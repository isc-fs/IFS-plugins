# ISC Formula Student Racing Team: marketing context

Read this before producing anything public.

## Who we are

ISC Formula Student Racing Team is the official Formula Student team of Universidad
Pontificia Comillas (ICAI), Madrid. Students design, build and race an electric
prototype each season and compete internationally.

- Season car naming: `IFSxx`. IFS08 was 2025/26, IFS09 is 2026/27.
- Category: Electric Vehicle, with a Driverless programme.
- Departments: Driverless, Control Electronics, Telemetry, Powertrain, Chassis, plus
  Project Management, Marketing and Sponsorship.
- Recent competitions: Formula Student Austria and Formula Student Spain 2026.
- A rebrand using the Comillas coat of arms lion motif has been proposed. Check the
  Brand System page in Notion for whether it was adopted before using it anywhere.

## Audiences, ranked when they conflict

1. **Prospective sponsors.** They fund the season. They need evidence of a serious
   engineering team, not enthusiasm.
2. **Prospective members.** They are the team's continuity. They need to see work
   worth spending a degree on.
3. **Current sponsors.** They need visible proof of what they bought.
4. **The wider FS community.** They reward real engineering content and see through
   spin instantly.

## Tone

Concrete over enthusiastic. Our credibility comes from engineering detail, not
adjectives. A post that says which sensor, at what sample rate, and why that mattered
beats one that says the team is thrilled to announce a milestone.

Never:

- Invent statistics, follower counts, lap times, or placement results.
- Claim a part is built when only a render exists.
- Spin a bad result. The FS community competes against us and knows what happened.
- Use a sponsor's logo or name before the agreement is signed.

## Language

- LinkedIn: English.
- Instagram and TikTok: judgement per piece. Spanish reaches ICAI students better,
  English reaches the international FS community. Ask if unclear.
- Internal documents and the Notion workspace: always English.

## The capture constraint

The defining constraint of Formula Student content is that it is bottlenecked by
capture, not by writing. The car exists in each state exactly once. If nobody filmed
the layup, that content does not exist and cannot be made later.

This has a direct consequence for every skill here: when planning content, the question
is not what to post but what has to be captured, by whom, before which date.
